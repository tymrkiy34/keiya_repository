# Look mechanics

The paws, seated pelvis and torso stay planted on the same baseline. The neck carries a short broad head. The head pitches upward/downward and yaws toward viewer right/left; ears follow that physical head, never whole-body rotation. The nose tip, muzzle surface, pupil/iris surfaces and eyelids move together with the skull. Keep both eyes original dark brown, preserving the white blaze and black mask. Up exposes a little underside of the muzzle with pupils toward upper lid; down lowers nose and gaze toward paws with more forehead visible. Right/left turn muzzle and gaze clearly toward corresponding screen edge, retaining a visible readable face. Intermediates combine pitch and yaw in even clockwise steps. No rear-of-head views, disappearing face, pupil-only motion or turned torso.
